- 6 agent takes (Android, 4K 60 fps SDR, rotated) in the **3-bedder** showflat (958 sqft) — one of
  them silent b-roll of the bomb shelter (household shelter fitted as storage).
- 14 iPhone HDR HLG clips of the **4-bedder** showflat (no agent) — used in other edits, never under
  the agent's 3-bed claims.
- 3 brochure floor plans (3-bed C1 872 sqft, 4-bed D1 1,076 sqft, 5-bed premium E2P 1,324 sqft) with
  a key plan showing Holland Link / Holland Plain.

- Two reference reels: a cream serif "stat card" agent tour, and a dark motion-graphics reel.

## Feedback rounds that shaped the style
1. Captions + cards must match what he actually says (not generic floor-plan facts).
2. Open on "best value buy", then "958 sqft".
3. The third clip is the bomb shelter → cut to it on "bomb shelter".
4. No project name on the map / anywhere; CTA "Text me for the project name"; keep Holland Link.
5. Shelter on the left (he points to his right); last clip full-screen with details below him.
6. Tour too small → full-screen tour throughout, infographics blended over it.
7. Map too blended (twice) → solid cream map card with dark labels.
8. Floor plans on "3, 4, 5" — below the agent, not above.
9. Music bed under the reel; keep a voice-only cut for in-app trending audio.
10. Build it in FilmCraft so the edit stays editable (the earlier version rendered only an mp4).
    The FilmCraft export matched the ffmpeg render at every cue; captions became native caption
    tracks (no word-by-word highlight) in exchange for being retypeable in the editor.
