# Creative brief: asking the client

Ask after the research pass (`research-playbook.md`), so every question offers concrete,
researched options and a recommended default instead of a blank "what do you want?". Skip
anything the user has already answered or the files already show. Use the host's structured
question tool when it has one (Claude Code: `AskUserQuestion`, at most 4 questions per round,
recommendation first, previews for hooks and looks). Otherwise ask in chat as a short numbered
list. Plan on 2–3 rounds, and stop early once the brief is clear.

## The creativity dial
Offer this first, because it scopes everything else.

| Level | What the reel does | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** | Agent tour with clean captions, 2–3 stat cards, contact end card. Few transitions, no trend formats. | Conservative agents, luxury listings, a first video for a new client |
| **Bold** (Amberwood default) | Full-screen tour with motion infographics on his words: stacked titles, price bars, map card, floor plans popping in, full-screen b-roll cuts, ducked music bed. | Most new-launch and resale reels |
| **Experimental** | Bold plus a trend format (see below), speed ramps and whip or match cuts, intercut b-roll from other units (labelled), a punchier hook rewrite, alternate cuts to A/B test. | Saturated launches where every agency posts the same tour, or clients who want reach |

Trend formats you can propose at Experimental: "guess the price" reveal, POV ("POV: you just
upgraded from HDB"), a this-vs-that comparison (this unit vs the comparable), a 3-reasons
countdown, "things nobody tells you about this unit", a text-message CTA mock-up, a
day-in-the-life using area b-roll. Propose only formats the footage and transcript can
honestly support. An Experimental idea that needs extra shots goes on a "film this next"
list.

## Question bank
Pick from these, and pre-fill each option from research.

**Round 1: goal and audience**
- What is this reel for? DMs and leads, launch awareness, a resale listing, or the agent's personal brand.
- Who is the main buyer? Offer the 2–3 personas from research.
- Where will it be posted? Instagram Reels, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp status. This sets the length, safe zones and caption language.
- How creative should it be? Classic, Bold or Experimental.

**Round 2: story and claims**
- Which hook? Offer 2–3 hook options built from the top angles, each with its first-2-seconds
  text and the take it uses, as previews.
- Which selling points matter most? List the ranked angles and let them multi-select.
- Can the project name, developer, prices and comparables appear on screen? Check what the
  developer allows co-broke agents to show.
- Anything off-limits? Competitor names, a price, a unit type, certain footage.

**Round 3: look and sound**
- Brand constraints: agency colours, logo use, fonts, a house look.
- References: reels they like or dislike, and what exactly about each one.
- Music: an in-app trending track (deliver voice-only) or the built-in bed. What mood (warm house, cinematic, upbeat pop)?
- Captions: English only, or bilingual (e.g. English + 中文 for Xiaohongshu)? Caption style: keywords highlighted, or plain?
- Pace: natural, slightly slowed (`SPEED` 0.85–0.9), or relaxed (0.8)?

**Round 4: delivery** (often one question)
- Deliverables: how many cuts, which lengths (15 s teaser / 30–40 s main), which aspect ratios
  (9:16, or 4:5 for feed), cover frame, SRT, and voice-only and music versions.
- Deadline and who approves.

## The brief
Copy `assets/creative-brief-template.md` to `work/brief/creative-brief.md` and fill it in. Show it
to the user and get a yes before rendering. The section plan in SKILL.md step 5 is built from
the brief: the hook decides the take order, the selling points decide which graphics exist,
the dial decides how many effects. When later feedback changes a decision, update the brief
too, so it stays the record of what the client wants.
